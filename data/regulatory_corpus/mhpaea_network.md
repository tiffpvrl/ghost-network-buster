# MHPAEA — network composition (summary)

The Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act (MHPAEA) requires that financial requirements and treatment limitations applied to mental health or substance use disorder benefits be no more restrictive than those applied to medical/surgical benefits.

Network composition can be treated as a non-quantitative treatment limitation (NQTL). A plan that offers a materially narrower behavioral health provider network than its medical/surgical network may raise parity issues depending on plan design and enforcement context.

Consumer complaints often cite inability to find in-network behavioral health providers despite directory listings suggesting adequate access.
