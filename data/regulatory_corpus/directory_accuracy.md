# Provider directory accuracy (federal / state themes)

Accurate provider directories are foundational to network adequacy and surprise-billing protections. Federal and state rules increasingly require periodic verification of directory data, including removal of providers who are no longer in network or available.

Ghost listings — disconnected numbers, wrong networks, or clinicians no longer practicing — frustrate timely access to care and undermine directory-dependent consumer decision-making.

State departments of insurance and attorneys general have pursued enforcement where directories materially misrepresent available care, including behavioral health networks.
