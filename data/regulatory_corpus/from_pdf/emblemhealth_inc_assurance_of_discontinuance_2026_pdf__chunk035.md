97. Verification process review. The Independent Monitor shall: 
a. take a statistically valid random sampling of Participating Providers who were 
subject to Respondents’ Verification Process (the “Verified Providers”); 
b. compare the Participating Provider Information of the Verified Providers that is 
contained in Respondents’ Online Participating Provider Directory with current 
source documentation obtained through their Verification Processes and other 
quality control processes. The Independent Monitor may consult Respondents’ 
documentation, including claims data, applicable scripts, email notices, other 
correspondences and telephonic recordings, as well as independent data sources
