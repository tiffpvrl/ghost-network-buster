Comment: The Department received a comment to require residential treatment as an 
additional level of care in the provider directory.  
Response:  The regulation was previously revised to provide examples of level of care 
offered by a facility to be listed in the provider directory.  No changes were made to the 
regulation.

Comment:  The Department received a comment requesting clarification in the 
regulation that the specific names of designated staff are not required to be posted or 
listed publicly.  
Response: Due to prior comments requesting clarification of this requirement, the 
Department made a change in the revised proposed regulation that the MCO shall post 
the contact information for the department or unit, not the individual name of the 
designated staff member.  No changes were made to the regulation.
