Pursuant to the authority vested in the Commissioner of Health by section 4403 of the 
Public Health Law, Part 98 of Title 10 (Health) of the Official Compilation of Codes, 
Rules and Regulations of the State of New York is amended to be effective July 1, 2025, 
to read as follows: 
 
Subdivision (p) of section 98-1.13 is added to read as follows:  
A MCO shall meet standards for network adequacy and access standards for mental 
health and substance use disorder treatment services set forth in Subpart 98-5.
