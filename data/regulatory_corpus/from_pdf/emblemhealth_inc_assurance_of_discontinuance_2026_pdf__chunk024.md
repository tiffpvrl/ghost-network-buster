ii. Stratify Participating Providers into three groups: 
1. Providers who remain in the directory. These include providers who 
verified the accuracy of their directory information and do not fall within 
subsections (2) and (3) below. 
2. Providers who remain in the directory but require further investigation. 
Regardless of whether a Participating Provider attests to the accuracy of
