Response: MCOs are required to ensure provider directories are accurate, including if a 
provider is accepting new patients.  Public Health Law sets forth requirements that MCOs 
include provider responsibilities regarding the directories in their provider contracts. 
MCOs are responsible for making sure providers adhere to contractual obligations.   
Comment: The Department received a comment asking for clarity on what is meant by 
‘level of care’ offered by behavioral health provider.  
Response: The regulation was previously revised to provide examples of level of care 
offered by a facility to be listed in the provider directory. No changes were made to the 
regulation.  
Comment: The Department received a comment regarding MCO verification of the 
accuracy of the provider directory and that it should be multi-method (e.g., include email 
and other forms of electronic verification as well as telephonic verification). The 
regulation should be revised to clarify that these options are available and to eliminate the 
restriction to written verification. 
Response: The regulation does not specify the method MCOs should use to verify the 
accuracy of the information in the provider directory.  No changes were made to the 
regulation.

Comment: The Department received a comment that the requirement to review the 
accuracy of the provider directory ‘at least’ annually is insufficient.   
Response: The Department added ‘at least’ to the regulation language to specify that the 
MCO must conduct a review of the provider directory with behavioral health providers at 
least annually, however the Model Contract and NYCRR 98-1.16 has stricter 
requirements regarding maintaining and updating their overall Provider Directory that 
must be continued to be followed.  MCOs have discretion to choose the methods to verify 
their provider directories.
