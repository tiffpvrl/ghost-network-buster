Response: It is expected that MCOs will work with out-of-network providers as they do 
now when there is not an in-network provider to meet the health care needs of the 
enrollee.  It is further expected that a MCO will ultimately reach an agreement with an 
out-of-network provider.  Appointments may be scheduled by telehealth, unless the 
enrollee specifically requests an in-person appointment, which may facilitate access to a 
broader spectrum of providers.  If the out-of-network referral is not resolved to the 
enrollee’s satisfaction, the recourse is to submit a complaint to the Department.   
Comment: The Department received comments seeking clarity regarding the transition 
back to an in-network provider and grievance process.  

30 
 
Response: The regulation adopts a transition of care standard that recognizes the unique 
nature of behavioral health services.  If the MCO and provider deem that a transition 
would be harmful to the enrollee, the enrollee can continue to see the out of network 
provider.  If the enrollee or enrollee’s designee disagrees with determination, they may 
request an expedited determination or appeal pursuant to Public Health Law section 
4408-a or 4904, as applicable.  
Comment: The Department received a request to consider establishing provider 
obligations to ensure network adequacy since they are positioned to know their capacity 
limits and therefore should hold responsibility to aid MCOs. 
Response: The Department’s regulations direct the regulated MCO.   
Comment: The Department received comments stating that the regulations should be 
adopted as soon as possible.  
Response: The Department and New York State Department of Financial Services will 
align the effective date to July 1, 2025, in case the regulations are adopted on different 
dates.  This change has been made to 98-6.0.  
Comment: The Department received a comment requesting if a provider is not actively 
accepting new patients that they are removed from the directory.  
Response: MCOs are required to include if a provider is currently accepting new patients 
but should not remove them from the provider directory since they are still part of the 
MCOs network.
