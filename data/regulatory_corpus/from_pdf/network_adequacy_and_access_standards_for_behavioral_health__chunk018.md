Comment: The Department received comments regarding additional appointment types 
and wait time standards included in the Model Contract that are not included in this 
regulation.  
Response: The regulation implements a legislative mandate; however, MCOs with 
Medicaid lines of business will still be held to contractual obligations for appointment 
wait time standards for services outlined in the Medicaid Model Contract. No changes 
were made to the regulation.

Response: The Department has existing processes in place for monitoring compliance 
with access and availability.  The Department currently uses a 75% compliance rate and 
is increasing to 90% to align with the new federal requirement.  Surveillance standards 
are not included in this regulation, so no changes were made. 
Comment: The Department received a comment asking the reason that an initial 
appointment can’t include a health care professional who is “employed by or contracted 
with an outpatient facility or clinic”? This language appears to be an unnecessary 
restriction that will increase the difficulty of meeting already aspirational appointment 
wait times. 
Response: Section 98-5.5 of the regulation provides appointment wait time standards for 
both initial appointments within an outpatient facility or clinic as well as with a health 
care professional who is not employed by or contracted with an outpatient facility or 
clinic. No changes were made to the regulations.
