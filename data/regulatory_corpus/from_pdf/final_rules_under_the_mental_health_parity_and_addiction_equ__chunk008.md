Sunset of MHPAEA Opt-Out  
In these final rules, HHS finalizes regulatory amendments to implement the sunset provision for 
self-funded non-Federal governmental plan elections to opt out of compliance with MHPAEA, 
as adopted in the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2023.
