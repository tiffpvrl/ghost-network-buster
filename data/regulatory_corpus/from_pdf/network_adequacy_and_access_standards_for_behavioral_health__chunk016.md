RURAL AREA FLEXIBILITY ANALYSIS 
Types and Estimated Numbers of Rural Areas: 
 This rule applies uniformly throughout the state, including rural areas.  Rural 
areas are defined as counties with a population less than 200,000 and counties with a 
population of 200,000 or greater that have towns with population densities of 150 persons 
or fewer per square mile.  The following 44 counties have a population of less than 
200,000 based upon the United States Census estimated county populations for 2020 
(https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/).  Approximately 17% of small health care facilities 
are located in rural areas.
