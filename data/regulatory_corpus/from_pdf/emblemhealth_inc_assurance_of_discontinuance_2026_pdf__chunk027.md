ii. Consider any Access Complaint related to access to behavioral health services

accurate directories rests with providers. 
vi. If a Member lodges a Complaint, grievance, or appeal regarding surprise 
bills, Respondents must inform the Member about the requirements under 
New York law (11 NYCRR § 52.77) and federal law (42 U.S.C. § 300gg-

cost-sharing) when a plan provides inaccurate network status information.
